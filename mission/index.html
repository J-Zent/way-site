WAY.

WAY. exists as a reminder to live. Despite the fact that up to this point we have not ceased to live, it remains incredibly easy to forget the simple task of living. It is a reminder to so courageously say “here I am.” To first, prior to anything, live the reality of being beloved.

ζῆτε ἀγαπώμενοι

ζῆτε
live. Present tense and plural, so it means to keep on living, and it is said to more than one person.
ἀγαπώμενοι
being loved. Passive and ongoing: the love is received, and it does not stop.
